# LTX Video: Cinematic Action Sequences

This guide covers the workflows in our [LTX Video library](/workflows?category=video) — how to lock camera motion, keep a character consistent across cuts, and build a multi-clip action sequence instead of one isolated generation.

> ℹ️ **Which LTX model is this?** These workflows run on Lightricks' original **2B checkpoint (v0.9.5)** — the lightweight release that runs on 12–16GB cards. Lightricks shipped **LTX-2.5** (22B, native multishot, up to 4K HDR) in August 2026 as their new flagship. If you're on a 24GB+ card and want the best possible quality, start there instead — these lighter workflows are for everyone else, and for fast iteration even if you do have the VRAM to spare.

Already have ComfyUI running? Skip to [Step 1](#step-1--the-motion-scale-parameter). If not, follow [ComfyUI Complete Setup](/guides/comfyui-complete-setup) first — this guide assumes ComfyUI, ComfyUI-LTXVideo, and ComfyUI-VideoHelperSuite are already installed.

## Step 1 — The motion_scale parameter

Every LTX Video workflow in our library exposes a `motion_scale` value on the `LTXVSampler` node. This is the single biggest lever for how "cinematic" a clip feels:

| motion_scale | Feel | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 0.8 | Stable, minimal drift | [Fast Draft](/workflows/14) — validating a prompt before a full render |
| 1.0 | Neutral, default | General text-to-video, product shots |
| 1.2 | Cinematic momentum | [Cinematic 9:16](/workflows/12) — Shorts/TikTok pacing |
| 1.4 | Aggressive, kinetic | [Action Chase](/workflows/13) — chase and combat scenes |

Push past 1.4 and you'll start seeing warping artifacts on fast-moving edges — that's the ceiling for the 2B checkpoint. If you need more headroom before artifacts kick in, that's one of the areas LTX-2.5 improves on.

## Step 2 — Camera lock across a sequence

"Camera lock" here doesn't mean a literal camera node — LTX Video doesn't expose camera extrinsics directly. It means keeping the *scene* consistent from clip to clip so a cut reads as one continuous sequence instead of four unrelated generations. Three things control that:

1. **Fixed seed within a shot.** Changing the seed between takes of the *same* shot is why re-rolls look like a different scene. Lock the seed while you dial in a shot, only re-roll once you're ready to move to the next one.
2. **A consistent subject description.** Keep the character/vehicle/environment tokens identical across every clip in the sequence — put them first in the prompt, and don't paraphrase them between shots.
3. **A per-shot `motion_scale`.** Establishing shots read better at 1.0–1.2; the payoff shot (impact, near-miss, reveal) is where you push to 1.4.

## Step 3 — Build a 5-clip action sequence

Structure a short action beat as five 97-frame clips (~8 seconds each at 12fps, 768×1344 for vertical), each using the [Action Chase](/workflows/13) workflow with the same subject description and a shot-specific `motion_scale`:

| # | Shot | motion_scale | Prompt focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish | 1.0 | Wide shot, subject and environment, camera static |
| 2 | Build | 1.2 | Subject starts moving, environment starts blurring |
| 3 | Peak | 1.4 | Full-speed chase, GoPro POV cues, motion blur |
| 4 | Complication | 1.3 | Near-miss or obstacle, slight motion pullback |
| 5 | Resolution | 1.0 | Motion settles, camera steadies |

Render each clip separately, then assemble in any video editor (VideoHelperSuite exports each clip as MP4). Keep every clip's resolution and frame count identical so the cut doesn't jump.

## Step 4 — Pick the right workflow for your hardware

| Workflow | VRAM | Frames | Resolution | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Fast Draft](/workflows/14) | 12GB | 25 | 512×512 | Prompt testing, under 15s per render |
| [Basic T2V](/workflows/11) | 12GB | 25 | 768×512 | Single clips, landscape |
| [Cinematic 9:16](/workflows/12) | 16GB | 97 | 768×1344 | Shorts/TikTok, single shot |
| [Action Chase](/workflows/13) | 16GB | 97 | 768×1344 | High-motion shots, sequence building |

## Common issues

**Motion looks warped or smeary at high motion_scale** — you're past the 2B checkpoint's stable range. Drop to 1.2–1.3, or move to LTX-2.5 if your hardware supports it.

**Clips don't match across cuts** — check that the seed was re-rolled between shots (it should differ) but the subject description wasn't reworded (it shouldn't).

**"CUDA out of memory" on the 16GB workflows** — drop to the 12GB [Basic T2V](/workflows/11) workflow, or add `--lowvram` to your ComfyUI launch command.

**Nodes missing** — open ComfyUI Manager → "Install Missing Custom Nodes" to pull in ComfyUI-LTXVideo and ComfyUI-VideoHelperSuite.
