**SD 1.5 vs SDXL vs LCM vs Turbo vs Flux - Which should you use?**

---

## Overview

Different Stable Diffusion architectures exist for different purposes. Using the wrong type causes errors, poor quality, or incompatibility.

> **Evidence note:** Model size, precision, resolution, custom nodes, offloading, and the workflow graph determine whether a run fits or how long it takes. NeuralDrift does not treat the examples below as measured hardware requirements or performance promises. Check the model card and workflow dependencies, then use the [compatibility policy](/compatibility) and a small local test for your exact setup.

---

## 🎨 SD 1.5 (Stable Diffusion 1.5)

### Best For
- Fast generation
- Anime and stylized art
- Low VRAM GPUs (4-8GB)
- Beginners
- Experimentation

### Specifications
- **Resolution:** 512×512 native (can upscale)
- **Memory and speed:** Often lighter than newer families, but actual requirements depend on the checkpoint and graph.
- **File size:** Varies by checkpoint format and precision.

### When to Use
- ✅ Learning ComfyUI
- ✅ Quick iterations
- ✅ Anime/cartoon styles
- ✅ Low-end hardware
- ✅ Large batch generation

### When NOT to Use
- ❌ Photorealistic commercial work
- ❌ High-resolution output without upscaling
- ❌ Fine detail requirements

---

## 🎨 SDXL (Stable Diffusion XL)

### Best For
- Photorealism
- Commercial quality
- High-detail images
- Professional work

### Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1024×1024 native
- **Memory and speed:** Usually more demanding than older base models; verify the selected checkpoint and workflow rather than relying on a GPU-tier rule.
- **File size:** Varies by checkpoint format and precision.

### When to Use
- ✅ Photorealistic images
- ✅ Professional quality needed
- ✅ High-resolution requirements
- ✅ Commercial projects
- ✅ Better text rendering

### When NOT to Use
- ❌ Low VRAM (< 8GB)
- ❌ Need fast iteration
- ❌ Anime/stylized preferred
- ❌ Limited disk space

---

## ⚡ LCM (Latent Consistency Models)

### Best For
- Ultra-fast generation
- Real-time previews
- Rapid prototyping

### Specifications
- **Steps:** 2-8 (vs 20-50 normal)
- **Trade-off:** These approaches commonly target fewer-step generation, but output quality and runtime are configuration-specific.

### When to Use
- ✅ Concept exploration
- ✅ Quick drafts
- ✅ Real-time generation
- ✅ Testing compositions

### When NOT to Use
- ❌ Final quality images
- ❌ Fine details needed
- ❌ Professional work

---

## 🚀 Turbo Models

### Best For
- 1-step generation
- Maximum speed
- Experimentation

### Specifications
- **Steps:** 1-4
- **Trade-off:** These approaches target very short sampling schedules; suitability depends on the model, prompt, and output requirements.

### When to Use
- ✅ Instant feedback
- ✅ Composition testing
- ✅ Live generation demos

### When NOT to Use
- ❌ Any final output
- ❌ Client work
- ❌ Detailed images

---

## 🌟 Flux

### Best For
- Cutting-edge quality
- Best prompt following
- State-of-art results

### Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1024×1024+
- **Memory and speed:** Requirements vary substantially by model variant, quantization, resolution, and workflow. Treat any compatibility label as unverified until an execution record exists.
- **File size:** Varies by published variant and precision.

### When to Use
- ✅ Maximum quality needed
- ✅ Best prompt adherence
- ✅ High-end hardware available
- ✅ Complex compositions

### When NOT to Use
- ❌ Limited VRAM
- ❌ Need fast iteration
- ❌ Limited disk space
- ❌ Custom nodes (compatibility still growing)

## 🔧 Compatibility Guide

### LoRAs
- SD 1.5 LoRA → SD 1.5 checkpoint ONLY
- SDXL LoRA → SDXL checkpoint ONLY
- Flux LoRA → Flux checkpoint ONLY

**Mixing = Errors or crashes**

---

### ControlNet
- SD 1.5 ControlNet → SD 1.5 models
- SDXL ControlNet → SDXL models

---

### VAE
- Most SD 1.5 models: Need external VAE
- SDXL: Usually has built-in VAE
- Flux: Custom VAE handling

## 🎯 Recommended Starting Point

### For Beginners
**SD 1.5** - Learn workflow basics fast

**Recommended checkpoint:**
- `realisticVision_v51.safetensors` (realism)
- `anything-v5.safetensors` (anime)

---

### For Intermediate Users
**SDXL** - Professional quality

**Recommended checkpoint:**
- `sd_xl_base_1.0.safetensors`

---

### For Advanced Users
**Flux** - Cutting edge

**Requirement:**
- RTX 4080 or better
- 24GB+ VRAM

## 🆘 Model Loading Errors

### "Model failed to load"
**Cause:** Wrong model type for workflow

**Fix:** Check workflow requirements, use matching model type

---

### "Unexpected key in state dict"
**Cause:** Model architecture mismatch

**Fix:** Verify exact model version needed (SD1.5 vs SDXL vs Flux)
